# Heap (Priority Queue) Tips & Notes
*   **Basics:** Tree-based structure satisfying the heap property (Min or Max at root).
*   **Java Specifics:** `PriorityQueue<Integer>` is Min-Heap.
*   **Tip:** Max-Heap requires passing `Collections.reverseOrder()` or `(a, b) -> b - a`.
*   **Tip:** Perfect for "Top K" or "Kth Largest" element problems.
